# Scenario 5: Order Workspace and Task Management

**Persona:** Sam Anderson — Laboratory Operations Coordinator

**Objective:** Explore the Myriad Order Workspace and understand how laboratory teams manage work, monitor order pipelines, and receive assigned work through role-based workspaces.

***

### Scenario

Throughout this lab, you've worked with individual orders and specimens as they progressed through the fulfillment process. In day-to-day operations, however, your teams need centralized places to monitor incoming work, manage priorities, and quickly identify the next items requiring attention.

In this exercise, you'll explore the Myriad Order Workspace and learn how ServiceNow provides role-based workspaces and intelligent work routing to help laboratory teams efficiently manage their workload.

***

1. Navigate to the **Myriad Order Workspace** home page by clicking the **home icon in the dark-colored left sidebar.**

#### What to Notice

The workspace provides a consolidated operational view of activity, including:

* **Order Pipeline metrics** showing where orders are within the fulfillment lifecycle.
* Lists of **My Active Tasks**, allowing users to focus on work currently assigned to them.
* **My Team's Tasks**, providing supervisors and managers visibility into work across their teams.
* Role-specific dashboards and operational metrics that help prioritize work and identify bottlenecks.

{% hint style="info" %}
Workspaces are fully configurable and can be tailored to the responsibilities of different users throughout the organization.

For example, an ordering provider, laboratory technician, quality analyst, dispatcher, supervisor, or laboratory manager can each have their own workspace with dashboards, lists, reports, and actions designed specifically for their role.

This ensures every user sees the information most relevant to their responsibilities while working from the same underlying platform and data model.
{% endhint %}

2. Review the pipeline cards displayed across the top of the workspace.

#### What to Notice

The pipeline provides an operational snapshot of orders currently progressing through each stage of fulfillment, allowing teams to quickly identify workload distribution and potential bottlenecks.

These metrics can be fully configured to reflect the milestones and KPIs most important to your organization.



3. Review Task Lists
4. Scroll through the task lists available on the workspace.

#### What to Notice

Notice the distinction between:

* **My Active Tasks** — Work currently assigned to you.
* **My Team's Tasks** — Work assigned across your team, providing supervisors with visibility into workload distribution and task progress.

These lists update dynamically as work is assigned, completed, or reassigned throughout the day.

5. Expand the **Inbox** panel located **in the dark-colored left sidebar.**
6. Change your status to **Available**.

#### What to Notice

When your status is set to **Available**, you become eligible to receive work through **Advanced Work Assignment (AWA)**.

Rather than manually searching for the next task, ServiceNow can intelligently route work directly to qualified users based on configurable routing rules.

Advanced Work Assignment (AWA) automatically distributes work to the appropriate users based on configurable business rules.

Organizations can route work using criteria such as:

* User skills or certifications.
* Assignment groups.
* Current workload or capacity.
* Task priority...

Once routed, users can accept work directly from their inbox, helping ensure tasks are distributed consistently while balancing workloads across laboratory teams
